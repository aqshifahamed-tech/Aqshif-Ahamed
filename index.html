About Aqshif

I started in graphic design where I discovered my passion for creating clean, visually appealing, and meaningful designs. As I continued learning, my interest expanded into full-stack web development, allowing me to combine creativity with technology to build modern, responsive, and functional websites.

I'm currently a student who is continuously learning and improving my skills. Although I've completed only a small number of projects, they have been built for myself and friends, giving me valuable hands-on experience in both design and development. Every project has helped me grow, solve real problems, and refine my approach.

I enjoy turning ideas into digital experiences, whether it's designing eye-catching visuals or developing user-friendly websites. My goal is to keep learning, take on more challenging projects, and build solutions that are both visually impressive and technically reliable.
